## Metadata of media files in Properties (after 0.1.3)
Idea, not started. The Properties window of an image, a sound, a video (and, later, a document or a font) shows, under the general lines it has, **the metadata the file carries**, in words and in the language of the interface, with a way to see everything raw. **Read-only here** (editing the EXIF of an image is in "Images", and a tag editor for sound is not planned). One **reader of metadata** (`core/metadata/`, plain TypeScript, no Electron, tested next to the code) is shared by this window, by the metadata panel of the image editor ("Metadata (EXIF)") and by the track titles, artists and covers of the playlists.

What is shown, by kind (only what exists in the file; a row with no value is not drawn)
- **Images**: width and height, bit depth, colour type and **colour profile** (ICC name), resolution (dpi), whether it is animated (frames and loops of a GIF, WebP or APNG), and the **EXIF**: camera make and model, lens, date taken, exposure time, aperture, ISO, focal length, flash, orientation, software, artist, copyright, description, and the **GPS** position (shown as text and **flagged as a location** so the person knows before sharing the file, with Copy but no map: nothing leaves the computer); **XMP** (title, creator, rights, keywords, rating) and **IPTC** (caption, keywords, credit); PNG `tEXt`/`iTXt` (title, author, comment, and the long `parameters` text that picture generators write) and the JPEG comment; an SVG's size, `viewBox`, `<title>` and `<desc>`; the sizes inside an ICO
- **Sound**: container and codec, duration, **bit rate** (constant or variable), sample rate, channels (mono, stereo, 5.1), bit depth, and the **tags**: title, artist, album, album artist, track and disc number, year or date, genre, composer, comment, lyrics, ReplayGain, encoder, and the **cover** (a small picture, its type and size), from ID3v1 and v2, Vorbis comments (FLAC, Ogg, Opus), APE, MP4/M4A atoms and the `INFO` chunks of a WAV
- **Video**: container, duration, the **video stream** (codec and profile, width and height, frame rate, bit rate, aspect, colour, rotation), each **audio stream** (codec, language, channels, sample rate), the **subtitle** tracks and **chapters**, the creation date, the title and tags, the encoder and, in a phone's video, the **location** (flagged as for an image)
- **Later, by the same window**: a **PDF** (title, author, subject, keywords, creator, producer, dates, number of pages, page size, version, whether it is encrypted or tagged, from pdf.js's `getMetadata()` that is already there), an **Office or OpenDocument file** (title, author, last modified by, dates, revision, pages, words, the application), an **EPUB** (title, author, language, identifier, publisher), a **font** (family, style, version, designer, licence, number of glyphs), an executable's header (the machine, the type: with the hex view's header panel)

How it is shown
- [ ] **Properties** gets sections under the general rows (Image, Sound, Video, then Document), each a two-column list; the general part opens at once and the metadata **fills in** (a quiet "reading…", then the rows, or "No metadata found", or "This file could not be read: it may be damaged" in plain words); a **Show All Tags** fold lists every tag raw with its name and value, a **Copy** on a row and **Copy All as Text**; a long value (lyrics, a comment, a generator's parameters) is a fold with a scroll; the **cover** of a sound and the **thumbnail** of an image are small pictures made from the file's own bytes (no network); a file with a location says so at the top
- [ ] Values are written for people: durations as `3:41` or `1:02:07`, a bit rate as `320 kbps (constant)`, a sample rate as `44.1 kHz`, channels as `Stereo`, an aperture as `f/2.8`, an exposure as `1/250 s`, a date in the language's format (the raw value on demand); every label and enumeration is in English and Brazilian Portuguese (the existing test that both languages have the same keys covers them)
- [ ] The same window for a file **inside a ZIP** or a **snapshot** (a stored entry is read by range; a compressed one is read from its start up to what the reader needs, with a limit, and said when it is too big), and for the **media tab**: an **Info** button on the toolbar of a sound or a video, and of an image, opens it
- [ ] A **summary line** in the status bar for the file in front when it is a picture, a sound or a video (`4032 × 3024`, `3:41 · 320 kbps`), from the same reader and its cache

How it is read (the decisions are the engineering ones)
- The readers run in a **Web Worker of the interface** (no file system, no Node: a parser of files from anywhere is the riskiest code of the application, so it lives in the sandbox), fed by **`fb:read-range`** windows: only the **head** of the file (the first 64 KiB to 1 MiB, more when an ID3 tag or a big `moov` says so), and the **tail** where a format keeps its index (ID3v1 and APE at the end of an MP3, an MP4 whose `moov` box is last); never a whole file, never more than a limit, and a **time limit** per reading
- **Own small readers or libraries** (a spike with numbers written here): `exifr` (MIT, small, fast: EXIF, XMP, IPTC, ICC) or our own IFD reader shared with the EXIF editor; for sound and video **`music-metadata`** (MIT, wide: tags, covers, containers; large) or **`mediainfo.js`** (MediaInfo in WebAssembly, BSD-2: the most complete, with codecs, streams and chapters, a couple of MB of `.wasm` that goes in the CSP's allowed list for the worker); our own for PNG, GIF, BMP, ICO, WebP and the small RIFF/ID3/Vorbis/MP4 cases keeps the package small
- Results are cached by file and version (path, size, modified time) in a small LRU, so reopening Properties, the status bar and a playlist do not read twice; reading stops when the window closes

Safety, performance and tests
- A length in a file is never trusted: every box, chunk, frame and tag size is checked against the room left and a depth limit; a loop of atoms that points to itself, a size of 4 GiB in a 100-byte file, a truncated file and a tag with a million frames all end in a refusal in plain words, never a freeze or a crash; the **hostile fixtures** (`fixtures/hostile.ts`) get media cases; fuzzed inputs in the unit tests
- [ ] Tests: hand-made files from the fixture builders (PNG with text chunks, a JPEG with EXIF/XMP/IPTC, a WAV with `INFO`, an MP3 with ID3v2.3, v2.4 and v1 and a cover, a FLAC with Vorbis comments and a picture, an MP4 with `moov` first and last, a minimal Matroska, an ICO, an animated GIF), each read back to the exact values; the labels in both languages; the dialog's states (reading, rows, none, damaged); the GPS flag; and an end-to-end spec: Properties of a picture and of a sound of known tags in a folder and inside a ZIP. A budget: the window opens at once and the metadata of a 4 GB video appears in about a second (range reads, not the file)
- Decisions to make before this is started: **the readers** (own and small, or `music-metadata` and `mediainfo.js`: the spike decides: which first?); **GPS** shown as text with a flag (the recommendation) or hidden until asked; **groups**: images and sound first (they share the readers of the EXIF panel and the playlists), then video, then documents and fonts; and whether the **status bar summary** and the **Info button** are wanted now or later
